About the Student Risk Prediction System

Empowering Educators with Data-Driven Insights

🎯 System Overview

The Student Risk Prediction System is a machine learning-powered tool designed to help educators identify students who may be at risk of academic failure. By analyzing various academic and personal factors, the system provides early warning indicators that enable timely intervention and support.

This proactive approach allows teachers and administrators to:

  • Identify struggling students before critical performance issues develop
  • Allocate resources and support to students who need it most
  • Monitor progress and adjust interventions based on data
  • Improve overall student outcomes and graduation rates

🔍 How It Works

The system uses a machine learning model trained on historical student data to predict academic risk. The model analyzes key factors that influence student success:

Academic Performance

First and second period grades provide direct insight into current academic standing

Past Failures

History of course failures indicates students who may struggle with course material

Attendance

Number of absences correlates with engagement and course completion

Study Time

Weekly study hours reflect student commitment and academic engagement

Family Support

Family involvement and support significantly impacts academic success

Technical Access

Internet access and school support services enable effective learning
